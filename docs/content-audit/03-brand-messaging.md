## 4. Brand Voice

**Tone:**

- Professional, authoritative, practical
- Clear and direct; avoids jargon without dumbing down
- Confident without arrogance
- Human; acknowledges real workplace constraints
- Outcome-focused; emphasizes impact over process

**Style Guidelines:**

**Do:**

- Use short, declarative sentences
- Lead with benefit, then process
- Use active voice ("We help you..." not "Safety solutions are provided...")
- Speak to the visitor's need ("Protect your team" not "HSE best practices")
- Cite specific examples and proof points
- Use British English consistently (programme, organisation, centre, etc.)
- Acknowledge trade-offs ("Safety works best when integrated into operations, not added as separate overhead")

**Avoid:**

- Vague corporate language ("world-class", "innovative", "synergies")
- Repetitive safety clichés ("Safety is our priority", "We care about your team")
- Passive voice ("Safety should be maintained..." vs "Keep your team safe...")
- Marketing hyperbole ("Revolutionizing", "Transforming", "Game-changing")
- Assumed familiarity with HSE terminology
- Exceeding 50 words in body paragraphs where possible
- Exclamation marks outside direct CTAs

**Example Voice in Action:**

❌ _Poor:_
"Dune Consulting delivers innovative, world-class HSE solutions designed to transform your organisation's safety culture and drive meaningful behavioural change through best-in-class training and support services."

✅ _Better:_
"Dune Consulting helps your team recognize real risk, make safer decisions and contribute to stronger operations through practical training tailored to how you actually work."

### CTA Button Styling

- **Primary buttons** (strong color, high contrast): Main service CTAs, hero CTAs, final conversion CTAs
- **Secondary buttons** (lighter color, lower contrast): Navigation, "Learn More", exploratory CTAs
- **Link-style CTAs** (text only, arrow icon): Footer links, inline navigation, related content

### CTA Placement Rules

1. **Every page must have at least one primary CTA** (ideally in hero or above fold)
2. **Service detail pages must have CTA after benefits section** (before FAQ)
3. **Long pages (About, Mentorship) must have CTA at midpoint and end**
4. **Contact page must have form-focused CTA** (not external link)
5. **No more than 3 primary CTAs per page** (reduces decision paralysis)
6. **Related service CTAs should appear at bottom of detail page** (encourage cross-sell)

### Proof-Point Usage Rules

1. **Always verify before publishing** – Every number, year, statistic must be confirmed by client
2. **Use specific figures over generics** – "700+ professionals trained" is stronger than "Thousands of professionals trained"
3. **Use context in proof points** – "700+ professionals trained across 30+ corporate training sessions" gives fuller picture
4. **Separate corporate and mentorship metrics** – Don't mix "700+ trained (corporate)" with "320+ mentorship registrations" without clear labels
5. **Use proof points where most credible** – Stats on homepage/services pages, case studies on portfolio pages, mentor bios on mentorship page
6. **Never fabricate proof points** – If figure is unverified, don't use it; ask for verification
7. **Update statistics when data changes** – Maintain accuracy; outdated figures damage credibility
8. **Link statistics to outcomes** – Don't just say "700+ trained"; explain impact ("700+ professionals trained, operating safely across Nigeria and beyond")

  - Use **Event** schema only if page describes an actual event (date, location, attendees, activities)
  - Use **Person** schema only for verified founder/team member profiles
  - Use **Service** schema for project descriptions positioned as consulting work
  - Use **Organisation** schema for organizational context (e.g., client organization)
  - Note: Do not prescribe Event schema for all portfolio pages; evaluate actual content
- **Mentorship page:** Course schema (if programme describes learning outcomes, duration, instructor)
- **Contact page:** (optional) LocalBusiness schema

**Implementation Rule:** Structured data type must match the actual content and claim on the page. Do not use Event schema for pages that don't describe events.
